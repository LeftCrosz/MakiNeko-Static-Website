Frequently Asked Questions - Billing

What payment methods can I use to pay?

We accept PayPal, debit or credit card, Visa, Bank Transfer, Touch 'n Go Ewallet and GrabPay. If you want to use other payment methods, you can contact us.

How to view the total amount that I need to pay?

You can view it in the cart page by clicking the cart icon on the top right corner of the page.

What should I do if there is payment error?

You can contact us through email or live chat.

Can I request for refund?

We will evaluate your situation to decide whether the refund is applicable or not. Please review our refund policy to get a more clearer information.
