Return Policy

You can return or exchange a product if you change your mind or no longer want it. However, you need to pay for the return shipping costs. It is not compulsory to provide a reason, but we encourage you to provide us with a reason for the return or exchange to help us improve our product's quality. If you received a damaged or defective product, please contact our customer support through live chat or email us. We will return or exchange without any additional cost for you. However, it is the responsibility of the customer to pay for the return shipping costs unless the return is due to a defect or damaged product.
