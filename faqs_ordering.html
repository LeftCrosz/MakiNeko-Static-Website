Frequently Asked Questions - Ordering

How can to place an order?

You can go to the products page and click "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" button to add it to your cart.

I can't place order what should I do?

You can ask for help in our live chat support. We will assign a staff to help you. You could also email us or phone us to purchase a product.

Is my payment information secured?

Yes. Your payment information is secured. Your data is encrypted by our security system.

Can I cancel my order that is placed?

To change or cancel an order, please contact our customer support through Live Chat or email.

How long will it take to receive my order?

The delivery time is vary depending on the product's stock and your location. You will see estimated delivery time displayed in the checkout process. You will get notified when the product is ready to ship to your location.

How can I track my order?

You can go to your email to get the order status.
